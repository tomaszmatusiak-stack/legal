  • Gem packs (consumable) — Handful of gems (100), Pouch of gems (320), Chest of gems (750) and Vault of gems (1800). Gems are credited to your balance immediately.
  • Chef's Pass (one-time, non-consumable) — permanently adds one product slot, doubles the daily bonus, doubles the coins earned after every level and gives the table a golden rim. It does not unlock any level or location that cannot be reached through play.

Gems are a virtual in-game currency. They can be spent in the Game (for example on booster charges, skipping an order, extra time after a failed level, doubling a level reward or exchanging for coins) and have no monetary value outside the Game. They cannot be transferred, exchanged for money or withdrawn. Gems can also be earned through play without any purchase.

A consumable purchase (gems) is delivered immediately and is used up as you spend it; it cannot be restored once consumed. Non-consumable purchases (Starter pack, Chef's Pass) are tied to your store account and can always be brought back with "Restore purchases" (Settings, and the bottom of the Gems tab in the Shop) — also after reinstalling the app or switching devices.

Because the Game has no server, we cannot restore coins, gems or progress lost through deleting the app, resetting the save or losing the device. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.
